Additional Research

Two further analyses from Kyaw Soe Lwin extend this work: a leakage-free predictive risk model (F1 0.969, using only intake-available signals — income, household size, and location) and a guaranteed-income lever analysis — the only modeled intervention that reaches under-$50k households. Full write-up in Additional Findings.

With review and feedback from Richard, who also built One Raise Away, a companion app on rent-raise dynamics and early storage-auction signals.
